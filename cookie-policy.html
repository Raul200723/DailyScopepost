Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This page explains how DailyScopePost uses cookies and similar technologies, and the choices available to you through our consent banner.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that let a website remember information about your visit, such as preferences or, with consent, analytics and advertising identifiers.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic site functionality, such as remembering your cookie consent choice. These don't require consent under most privacy frameworks.
  • Analytics cookies: Used, where enabled, to understand aggregate site traffic and improve content.
  • Advertising cookies: Used to support advertising served through Google AdSense, including cookies Google and its advertising partners use to personalize ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

Your choices

When you first visit DailyScopePost, a cookie banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser's settings at any time. Rejecting non-essential cookies will not affect your ability to read articles.

Consent architecture

This site's cookie banner is intentionally lightweight and is built so a full consent management platform (CMP) can be integrated later without redesigning the page — useful if broader compliance requirements (such as GDPR or additional U.S. state privacy laws) apply to your situation.

Questions

Questions about our use of cookies can be sent to hello@dailyscopepost.com.
