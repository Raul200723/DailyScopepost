About DailyScopePost

Last updated: August 9, 2026

DailyScopePost is an independent digital publication covering United States news, politics, business, technology, and the explainers behind the headlines. We publish for a general U.S. audience that wants clear, factual coverage without sensationalism.

What we cover

Our coverage spans nine core areas: US News, Politics, Business, Technology, Science, Health, World, Money, and Lifestyle. Alongside coverage of current events and institutions, we maintain a dedicated Explainers section — evergreen, plain-English guides to how things like the Federal Reserve, the Electoral College, or a 401(k) actually work. We built this section deliberately: news moves fast, but the systems behind it don't change nearly as often, and we think readers deserve context that outlasts a single news cycle.

Our audience

DailyScopePost is written primarily for readers in the United States who want straightforward, non-partisan information they can act on — whether that's understanding a policy debate, a market move, or a personal finance decision.

How we select stories

We prioritize stories and topics that are directly relevant to how our readers live, vote, work, and spend — not stories chosen purely because they're likely to generate clicks. We favor substance over speed: we would rather publish a clear, well-structured explainer a day later than rush out a thin, confusing one.

How we verify information

Our explainers are grounded in information from primary and authoritative sources — government agencies, established research institutions, and official documentation — rather than secondhand summaries. Every article goes through human editorial review before publication, overseen by our editorial staff. See our full Fact-Checking Policy for details on this process.

Our use of AI-assisted workflows

Some DailyScopePost content is drafted with the assistance of AI tools as part of our internal workflow, and every piece is reviewed and edited by our editorial staff before it's published. We disclose this openly rather than presenting AI-assisted content as traditional human field reporting. Read more in our Editorial Policy.

How corrections work

When we get something wrong, we correct it and note the correction transparently. Our Corrections Policy explains exactly how that process works.

How to reach us

We welcome feedback, corrections, and story suggestions from readers. Visit our Contact page for the right way to reach our editorial team.
