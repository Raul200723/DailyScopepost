Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DailyScopePost collects, how it may be used, and the choices available to you as a reader.

Information we may collect

  • Usage data: Where analytics tools are enabled, we may collect information about how visitors use the site, including pages viewed, device and browser type, and general location derived from IP address.
  • Information you provide directly: If you contact us by email, we collect the information you choose to include in that message, such as your email address and its contents.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for essential site functionality and, with your consent, for analytics and advertising purposes, including through Google AdSense. See our Cookie Policy for details.

How we may use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website.
  • To understand aggregate traffic and content performance.
  • To respond to inquiries sent to our contact addresses.
  • To support advertising served through Google AdSense.

Advertising and Google AdSense

DailyScopePost works with Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites. Visitors can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage their ad personalization settings at Google's Partner Sites policy.

Third-party services

This site may use Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and Google AdSense. These services collect and process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not control how third-party services process data beyond the settings we configure.

Your choices

You can decline non-essential cookies through our cookie consent banner, which controls analytics and advertising cookies. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies directly through browser settings. Google also provides its own Ads Settings tool for managing ad personalization directly.

Children's privacy

DailyScopePost is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@dailyscopepost.com.
